# Use Cases

A tour of what DClaw Vendor does, module by module. Every AI action runs against
the configured LLM (Ollama by default).

## 1. Manage vendors & purchase orders
Create vendors and POs, track line items and receive quantities, filter the
directory by status / category / tier, and see totals roll up automatically.

## 2. Ask the AI Copilot
The floating Copilot answers questions grounded in your actual data ("which
vendors have open POs over $10k?") and suggests next actions. One-click **Evaluate
(AI)** on any vendor returns risk flags, a performance outlook, and a
recommendation.

## 3. Classify & enrich the directory
**AI Classify** assigns a spend category, industry, and strategic tier to vendors
in bulk. **Enrich** pulls a company profile (size, HQ, founding year) from the
vendor's website — or infers one — and records provenance.

## 4. Onboard a new vendor
Spin up an onboarding case, generate an **AI document checklist**, upload documents
and **AI-validate** them against their expected type, then route through a
multi-step approval chain. Approving the last step lets you **activate** the vendor.

## 5. Track performance
Generate a 10-KPI scorecard across quality, delivery, cost, and compliance. See the
overall composite, peer **benchmark** (percentile within the same category), and a
score **trend** over periods.

## 6. Assess risk & manage contracts
Run a risk assessment across 20 risk types; re-running surfaces **change alerts**
(new/escalated/resolved). Store contracts, **extract key terms** from pasted text
with AI, and watch the **renewals** list for expiring agreements.

## 7. Analyse spend & reconcile invoices
The **Analytics** page breaks spend down by category/vendor, finds **~10% savings**
opportunities with AI, reports supplier-diversity spend, and **syncs** an ERP to
**reconcile invoices** against PO totals (matched / over- / under-billed / unmatched).

## 8. ESG, feedback & compliance
Score vendor **sustainability** (E/S/G + carbon footprint + targets), run
stakeholder **surveys** with AI sentiment and trend, and schedule **audits** with
finding tracking and closure validation (an audit can't close with open findings).

## Who it's for
- **Procurement teams** consolidating spend and de-risking suppliers.
- **Compliance/ESG** owners tracking diversity, sustainability, and audits.
- **Finance** reconciling POs and invoices against the ERP.
